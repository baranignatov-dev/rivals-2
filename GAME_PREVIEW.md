# RIVALS 2 - GAME PREVIEW

## 🎮 Your Game Overview

Your Rivals 2 game is a **50-player multiplayer combat arena** with epic battles, multiple game modes, and unique characters!

---

## 📊 Game Statistics

- **Max Players:** 50
- **Game Modes:** 6 (1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, 5v5, Free For All)
- **Maps:** 3 (Crystal Canyon, Volcano Valley, Sky Fortress)
- **Characters:** 4 (Blade, Nova, Titan, Omega)
- **Bosses:** 2 (Crystal Dragon, Omega Titan)
- **Round Duration:** 5-10 minutes
